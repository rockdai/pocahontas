pocahontas logo

White square background (#FFFFFF), one centered black uppercase P (#000000).
Typeface: Contrail One by Riccardo De Franceschi / Sorkin Type Co.
Google Fonts: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Contrail+One
Font source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/contrailone
Font SHA-256: a7665eddb068507736f593ca8eb9f547db8a8d61ea1cf03e072554aab5a0e8b1
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1; see fonts/OFL.txt.

The official family provides Regular (400, normal) only. This mark uses the
original uppercase P outline, a 12 px black stroke for synthetic bold, and
a 12-degree rightward shear for oblique styling. No font substitution.
Canvas: 1024 × 1024 px. Glyph outline height: 704 px before emboldening.

Generate both assets on macOS from the repository root:
  swift scripts/generate-logo.swift

pocahontas.svg contains glyph outlines and an opaque white background; it
does not require the font to be installed. pocahontas.png is opaque RGB.
